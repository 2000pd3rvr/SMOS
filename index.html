What SmOS does

Service modes

Delivery, collection, or sitting in — each merchant module configures what guests can choose.

Two ways to order

Browse a structured menu, or say what you want in plain language. SmOS matches dishes to the kitchen list.

Merchant modules

Every venue gets a named customisation (e.g. SmOS_CC) with its own menu, copy, and fulfilment rules — without bloating the public website.

Extensible core

Shared platform chrome and APIs; voice, kitchen dashboards, and payments plug in as the stack grows.
